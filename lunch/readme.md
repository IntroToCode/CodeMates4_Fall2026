# Mediterranean Chicken & Quinoa Bowl

## Description

A fresh, healthy, and filling lunch bowl with seasoned chicken, fluffy quinoa, crunchy vegetables, feta cheese, and a simple lemon dressing. Perfect for meal prep!

Ladle & Leaf makes a Mediterranean Chicken & Quinoa Bowl inspired by the flavors and ingredients of Mediterranean cuisine, particularly Greek cooking. While it's not a traditional dish from one specific country, it combines staples like olive oil, lemon, oregano, fresh vegetables, and feta cheese. This is a team favorite on Berkeley campus.

What makes it special is its balance of fresh flavors, nutrition, and convenience. It's high in protein, full of colorful vegetables, and easy to customize. It's also great for meal prep, making it a healthy and satisfying lunch option for busy days.

### Ingredients:
1 cup dry quinoa
2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
1 cucumber, diced
1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
1/4 red onion, diced
1/3 cup crumbled feta cheese
2 tablespoons olive oil
1 lemon, juiced
1 teaspoon dried oregano
1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
Salt and pepper to taste
Optional: hummus, avocado, or kalamata olives
      
<!-- List every ingredient -->

### Steps for preparation:

1. Rinse quinoa and cook according to package instructions (about 15 minutes).
2. Season chicken with oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook chicken for 5–7 minutes per side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
4. While the chicken cooks, dice the cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion.
5. Whisk together the remaining olive oil and lemon juice with a pinch of salt and pepper.
6. Divide quinoa between two bowls. Top with sliced chicken, vegetables, and feta.
7. Drizzle with lemon dressing and serve!

### Notes:
Great for meal prep! Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3–4 days.
Substitute chickpeas for chicken for a vegetarian option.
Add hummus or tzatziki for extra flavor.
Keep dressing separate until ready to eat.
